Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

TL;DR

Xeno does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on remote servers. All configuration, widget settings, home screen layouts, and preferences are stored locally on your device only.

What Data Does Xeno Collect?

Xeno stores the following data entirely on your device in its private application directory:

  • Home screen layout & configuration
  • Widget settings
  • User preferences (themes, gestures, icon packs, etc.)
  • App drawer state
  • Preset/backup exports (generated on-demand)

What Xeno Does NOT Collect

Xeno does not:

  • Collect or transmit personal information (name, email, phone, location, etc.)
  • Send analytics, crash reports, or telemetry
  • Track app usage or home screen activity
  • Require cloud sign-in or account creation
  • Integrate with third-party advertising or tracking services

Permissions Explained

Permission Why Needed
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Populate app drawer with installed apps.
CHANGE_CONFIGURATION Listen for system-wide config changes (theme, font size).
READ_CALENDAR Optional — only if using Calendar widget.
READ_CALL_LOG Optional — only if using call-log features.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Auto-launch on device restart.

Third-Party Services

Xeno does not use any third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking services. No data is sent to Google, Firebase, Mixpanel, or any external service.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • As long as you use Xeno: All data persists until you uninstall.
  • After uninstalling: Your data is automatically deleted (standard Android behavior).
  • Backups you create: Remain until you delete them manually.
